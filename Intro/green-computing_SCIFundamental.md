- **`description`** Learn the concepts needed to build, maintain and run greener applications
- **`abstract`**
Most carbon metrics tell you how bad things are. The SCI was designed to tell you what to do about it. This course introduces the Software Carbon Intensity (SCI) specification: a new standard built from the ground up to make software's carbon footprint transparent, comparable, and actionable.

## Audience & Prerequisites

**`audience`** (Audience)
- **`audienceType`** Anyone involved in the process of building, deploying, or managing software, anyone who wants to make their software applications more sustainable and build a career in green software

**`competencyRequired`**
- No prior knowledge of computing infrastructure required, ideally have at least one year of industry experience building, deploying, or managing software applications.

**`teaches`**
- Explaining why traditional carbon metrics fail to drive action in software teams
- Describing the two boundaries that every metric must align to drive the right actions
- Calculating operational and embodied emissions using the SCI equation
- Explaining why the SCI excludes market-based measures like offsets and RECs
- Selecting appropriate quantification methods based on available data
- Applying the SCI equation to real software systems
