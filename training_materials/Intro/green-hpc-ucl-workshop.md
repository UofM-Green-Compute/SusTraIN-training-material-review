## Audience & Prerequisites

**`audience`** (Audience)
- **`audienceType`** Anyone involved in using or delivering HPC resources — users, software developers for HPC, HPC service operators, and those involved in procuring HPC systems. No prior knowledge of environmental sustainability assumed.

**`competencyRequired`**
- Familiarity with key concepts in climate change and green software engineering (covered in the pre-workshop setup material)

**`teaches`**
- Understand carbon efficiency and its relationship to energy efficiency
- Know how HPC systems can potentially reduce emissions
- Understand carbon intensity of electricity generation and carbon-aware HPC use
- Appreciate embodied emissions associated with HPC hardware
- Be aware of frameworks for measuring and reporting carbon emissions in HPC contexts
- Gain practical advice on measuring and improving carbon efficiency of HPC use
