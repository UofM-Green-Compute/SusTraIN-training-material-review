## Descriptive Metadata

- **`name`** Green software use on HPC
- **`description`** An introduction to environmental sustainability principles in the context of high performance computing systems
- **`abstract`** This lesson introduces environmental sustainability principles in the context of HPC systems. It covers understanding the scale of emissions from different sources, quantifying emissions from HPC use, and practical examples using real data from the ARCHER2 facility. Topics include carbon efficiency, energy efficiency, carbon awareness, hardware efficiency, measurement, and reducing emissions.

## Audience & Prerequisites

**`audience`** (Audience)
- **`audienceType`** Anyone involved in using or delivering HPC resources — users, software developers, HPC service operators, and those involved in procuring HPC systems. No knowledge of environmental sustainability principles assumed.

**`competencyRequired`**
- Familiarity with the key concepts in climate change and green software engineering (covered in the setup section)

**`teaches`**
- Understand carbon efficiency and how it relates to energy efficiency, including cases where they can conflict
- Know how HPC systems can potentially reduce emissions
- Understand carbon intensity of electricity generation and implications for carbon-aware HPC use
- Appreciate embodied emissions associated with HPC hardware
- Be aware of frameworks for measuring and reporting carbon emissions in the context of HPC
- Gain practical advice on measuring and improving the carbon efficiency of HPC use
