About the project

Better practice, across the research lifecycle.

Research increasingly relies on data-intensive workloads, large-scale infrastructure, and machine learning. The choices made when designing, running, and maintaining these systems can make a meaningful difference.

SusTraIN supports researchers, digital research professionals, infrastructure managers, and students with practical training for environmentally sustainable computing. This page is sample homepage copy and can be replaced with the project’s final introduction, news, and guidance.
